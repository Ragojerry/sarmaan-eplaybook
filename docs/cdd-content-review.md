## 2. Contradictions and questions for the reviewers (text kept as written)

1. **Acronyms** (PDF 3 (slide 2)). 'LQAS' is spelled out as 'Lots Quality Assurance Sampling' (the usual term is 'Lot Quality Assurance Sampling') and 'SAE' as 'Severe adverse Reaction.' (usually 'Serious Adverse Event'). Kept as written.
2. **SMS changed to SMC (please confirm)** (PDF 7 (slide 6)). Slide 6 lists 'Seasonal Malaria Chemoprevention (SMS)'. Everywhere else the flipbook uses SMC, so this was treated as a typing error and shown as 'SMC' in the app (see the table above). 'TGE delivery platforms' was likewise shown as 'the delivery platforms'.
3. **Wording** (PDF 7 (slide 6)). The target age group sentence reads 'AZM Drug Administration is aimed protection children aged 1-59 months' (a word seems to be missing, e.g. 'aimed at protecting'). Kept as written.
4. **Age limit (contradiction)** (PDF 11 (slide 10), PDF 15 (slide 14)). Slide 10 says children 'older than 59 months' are not eligible; slide 14 says 'Children from 60 months and older' should not be given AZM. A child aged 59 months and some days falls differently under each wording. Both kept as written.
5. **Age bands overlap (contradiction)** (PDF 11 (slide 10), PDF 12 (slide 11)). Slide 10 gives '3 to 4 months' and '3 to 11 months' as two bands with the same first sign, so they overlap. The slide 11 picture uses '3 to 4 months' and '5 to 11 months' instead, which matches the dosing bands (1-2, 3-4, 5-11 months). Kept as written.
6. **Malnutrition (possible contradiction)** (PDF 16 (slide 15), PDF 18 (slide 17)). Slide 15 marks 'mild/severe/acute malnutrition' as not eligible and referred. Slides 12 and 14 exclude only Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM), and slide 17 sends Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) to the facility for counselling without saying whether AZM is given. Kept as written.
7. **Data tool (Kobo Collect vs DIGIT HCM)** (PDF 17 (slide 16)). The MUAC section (slides 16 and 39) says data go into Kobo Collect, while the rest of the flipbook records children in the DIGIT HCM app. Kept as written; please confirm which tool CDDs use for MUAC.
8. **MUAC age range** (PDF 17 (slide 16)). MUAC applies to children 6-59 months, while AZM eligibility is 1-59 months. Kept as written.
9. **HFW-only items in the CDD book** (PDF 22 (slide 21), PDF 26 (slide 25)). Slide 21 lists 'When to complete PV Form and Med Safety App for AZM (for HFWs only)', and the referral form on slide 25 has a part 'To be completed by the Health Facility Worker'. The CDD flipbook has no slide that teaches the PV form or Med Safety App. Kept as written.
10. **Step references** (PDF 34 (slide 33)). Slide 33 says 'Repeat steps 3,4,5 and 6' but the steps on that slide are not numbered, so it is not clear which steps are meant. Kept as written.
11. **Dosing table on the card (please check)** (PDF 36 (slide 35)). The two dosing tables and the health advice are transcribed from the card picture on slide 35, which is small. Read at high zoom they say: 1-2 months 2 ml, 3-4 months 3 ml, 5-11 months 4 ml; height 50-53 cm 2 ml, 54-64 cm 4 ml, 65-75 cm 6 ml, 76-86 cm 8 ml, 87-97 cm 10 ml, 98-109 cm 12 ml, 110-121 cm 14 ml, 122-129 cm 16 ml, 130-136 cm 19 ml, 137-140 cm 25 ml. The last two rows break the +2 ml pattern (19 ml, then 25 ml); please confirm against the dosing pole and the DIGIT HCM app. The last line of the health advice is cut off in the source ('...during all').
12. **House-marking codes** (PDF 37 (slide 36), PDF 38 (slide 37)). Slide 37 says the CDD team code has 4 digits, but every example uses '56708/021' (5 digits plus a further 3-digit group that the format does not explain). The photo on slide 36 shows 'AZM/26/1 0041/021/2/2/c', which has no round between the year and the team code in the same way. In the photo itself the year '26' is written over the picture; the underlying photo reads 'AZM/25/1'. Kept as written.
13. **Call-in age categories** (PDF 40 (slide 39)). For AZM alone the call-in categories are 1-11 and 12-59 months; for integrated campaigns they are 6-11 and 12-59 months, which leaves children 1-5 months out of the integrated count. Kept as written.
14. **Round numbers** (PDF 38 (slide 37), PDF 48 (slide 47), PDF 50 (slide 49)). The child record card records 'round 1 or 2' per year, while the house-marking format allows rounds '1 or 2 or 3 or 4 or 5 or 6'. Probably rounds per year versus rounds overall; kept as written.
15. **Module 5 title** (PDF 2 (slide 1), PDF 60 (slide 59)). The contents page calls Module 5 'Interpersonal Communication and Social and behaviour Change (SBC)'; its opening slide says 'Interpersonal communication and Social behaviour (SBC)'. The module name in the app uses the contents-page wording; both are kept in the text.
16. **Duplicated heading** (PDF 65 (slide 64)). The heading on slide 64 reads 'Safeguarding during AZM MDA and AZM MDA'. Kept as written.
17. **Safeguarding email address (please confirm)** (PDF 67 (slide 66)). The reporting address is printed as 'concenrns@malariaconsortium.org' in the text layer of slide 66; the slide image shows 'concenrns@' too. It is probably meant to be 'concerns@', but an email address cannot be corrected without confirmation, so it is kept exactly as printed. Please confirm the correct address.
