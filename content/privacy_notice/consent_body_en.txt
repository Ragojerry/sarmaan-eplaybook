MALARIA CONSORTIUM
SARMAAN II MOBILE PLAYBOOK (LEARNING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM)
PRIVACY NOTICE

1. Introduction

Malaria Consortium is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of individuals participating in its programmes and activities.

This Privacy Notice explains how Malaria Consortium collects, uses, processes, stores, shares and protects personal data through the SARMAAN II Mobile PlayBook, a mobile-based Learning Management System (LMS) established to support learning, training, capacity development, knowledge management and programme implementation under the SARMAAN II project.

The Mobile PlayBook incorporates digital learning functionalities and Artificial Intelligence (AI)-enabled features designed to facilitate learning, provide interactive responses, improve user experiences and support continuous improvement of the platform.

This Privacy Notice applies to individuals who register, access or participate in learning activities through the platform, including:
• Community Drug Distributors (CDDs).
• CDD Supervisors.
• Health workers, including Officers-in-Charge (OICs) of health facilities.
• Other authorised personnel involved in SARMAAN II project activities.

By accessing the platform, you are provided with information about how your personal data is processed, the purposes of processing, the circumstances under which your information may be shared and the rights available to you under applicable data protection laws.

2. Who We Are

Malaria Consortium is the Data Controller responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing personal data collected through the SARMAAN II Mobile PlayBook.

The organisation processes personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 and its internal data protection and information security policies.

For questions, concerns or requests relating to your personal data, you may contact the Data Protection Officer (DPO):
Email: dpo.ng@malariaconsortium.org

3. Whose Personal Data Do We Process?

This Privacy Notice applies to the following categories of users:
• Community Drug Distributors (CDDs) participating in SARMAAN II project activities.
• CDD Supervisors responsible for supervising and supporting community-level implementation.
• Health workers, including Officers-in-Charge (OICs) of participating health facilities.
• Other authorised individuals involved in project training, supervision, coordination and implementation.

4. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

Depending on your role and the functionality of the LMS, Malaria Consortium may collect and process the following categories of personal data.

A. Personal Identification and Profile Information
• Full name.
• Gender, where collected.
• Age or date of birth, where necessary.
• Phone number.
• Email address, where available.
• State, Local Government Area (LGA), ward and community of deployment.
• Health facility or assigned operational location.
• Volunteer or staff identification number.
• Designation or role within the SARMAAN II project.

B. Account and Registration Information
• LMS username and user identification details.
• Account registration and activation information.
• Login and authentication records.
• Account status and access history.

C. Learning and Training Information
• Courses and learning modules accessed.
• Training participation and attendance records.
• Assessment and examination responses.
• Quiz scores and completion status.
• Learning progress and performance records.
• Training certificates and learning achievements.
• Responses, questions, feedback and interactions submitted through the LMS, including its AI-enabled features.

D. Technical and Platform Usage Information
The LMS may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:
• Date and time of platform access.
• Platform activity and interaction logs.
• Device identification information and device type.
• Operating system information.
• Internet Protocol (IP) address.
• GPS or location information, where enabled and collected.
• Cookies, analytics and other tracking information.
• Technical error reports and diagnostic information.

E. Information Stored Through Offline Functionality
The Mobile PlayBook supports offline access to certain platform functionalities. Consequently, some information may be temporarily stored on the user's device and synchronised with the LMS when an internet connection becomes available.

Users should protect their devices against unauthorised access, particularly where LMS information is stored locally.

5. Why We Process Your Personal Data

Malaria Consortium processes your personal data for the following purposes:
1. User registration and account management: To create, verify, administer and maintain user accounts on the Mobile PlayBook.
2. Training and capacity development: To provide access to learning materials, training modules, educational resources and other relevant learning activities.
3. Learning assessment: To assess knowledge acquisition, monitor course completion and maintain training and assessment records.
4. Performance monitoring: To enable authorised supervisors and project management personnel to monitor learning participation, progress and completion.
5. Project monitoring and reporting: To generate training reports, measure capacity-building outcomes and support project implementation, monitoring and accountability.
6. Technical support and platform administration: To troubleshoot technical problems, maintain platform functionality, monitor system performance and improve the user experience.
7. Artificial Intelligence (AI) functionality: To facilitate AI-enabled learning interactions, respond to user questions, provide learning support and improve the relevance and effectiveness of the platform's AI functionalities.
8. LMS improvement and AI model training: To use relevant user interactions, responses, questions, feedback and learning-related information to evaluate, train, test and improve the LMS and its AI components for future improvement, subject to the applicable lawful basis and safeguards.
9. Project-related research: To use relevant information and responses generated through the LMS to support further research, analysis, evaluation and knowledge development relating to SARMAAN II and its public health objectives.
10. Compliance and accountability: To maintain appropriate records and demonstrate compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and programme requirements.

6. Use of LMS Responses for Artificial Intelligence Training and Improvement

The SARMAAN II Mobile PlayBook incorporates AI-enabled functionalities intended to support users in their learning and training activities.

When you interact with the AI components of the LMS, including by submitting questions, providing responses, entering information or using interactive learning features, the information you provide may be processed for the following purposes:
• To generate responses and provide learning assistance.
• To evaluate the accuracy, relevance and effectiveness of AI-generated responses.
• To identify gaps in learning content and areas requiring improvement.
• To train, test, evaluate and improve the AI models supporting the LMS.
• To enhance the platform's ability to provide relevant learning support in the future.
• To improve the overall quality, reliability and effectiveness of the Mobile PlayBook.

Your responses and interactions may therefore be used as learning and improvement inputs for the LMS and its AI components.

Where responses contain personal data, Malaria Consortium will ensure that an appropriate lawful basis is established for their use in AI training and improvement. Where consent is relied upon, the relevant consent will be obtained separately and transparently.

Where feasible, personal identifiers will be removed or the information anonymised before it is used for further model development and improvement. Access to identifiable or pseudonymised information will be restricted to authorised personnel and service providers who require it for approved purposes.

Your personal information will not be intentionally incorporated into publicly accessible AI training datasets without appropriate authorisation and safeguards.

7. Use of Information for Further Research

Information and responses generated through your participation in the Mobile PlayBook may also be used to support further research, analysis, evaluation and knowledge development relating to the SARMAAN II project and its associated public health objectives.

Such research may include:
• Assessing the effectiveness of digital learning and capacity-building interventions.
• Evaluating knowledge acquisition and training outcomes among CDDs, supervisors and health workers.
• Identifying knowledge gaps and areas requiring additional training.
• Analysing trends in learning participation and assessment outcomes.
• Supporting evidence-based improvements to malaria prevention, treatment and community health interventions.
• Generating insights and evidence to inform future programme design and implementation.

Where research findings are published, presented or otherwise disseminated, the information will be anonymised so that individual participants cannot reasonably be identified.

Research reports, publications, presentations and other knowledge products will therefore present findings in aggregated or anonymised formats.

Information will not be published in a manner that identifies individual CDDs, CDD Supervisors, OICs or other LMS users.

Where proposed research involves identifiable personal data or processing beyond the purposes described in this notice, Malaria Consortium will assess the applicable lawful basis and any additional ethical, regulatory and data protection requirements before proceeding.

Malaria Consortium processes personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Depending on the specific processing activity, the applicable lawful basis may include:

A. Consent
Where consent is the appropriate lawful basis, particularly for additional processing activities such as using identifiable user responses for AI model training or other optional purposes, Malaria Consortium will provide relevant information and obtain appropriate consent.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken before the withdrawal.

B. Legitimate Interests
Malaria Consortium may process personal data where necessary for its legitimate interests, including:
• Administering and improving the Mobile PlayBook.
• Monitoring learning participation and performance.
• Maintaining platform security and integrity.
• Improving training materials and learning outcomes.
• Supporting project monitoring, evaluation and accountability.

Such processing will be subject to an assessment to ensure that the organisation's legitimate interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.

C. Legal Obligations
Where necessary, personal data may be processed to fulfil applicable legal, regulatory or statutory obligations.

The lawful basis for each processing activity will be assessed and documented by Malaria Consortium.

The SARMAAN II Mobile PlayBook uses Amazon Web Services (AWS) as its cloud hosting infrastructure.

The platform's hosting and related data processing arrangements may involve AWS infrastructure located in African and European regions.

This means that personal data may be stored, processed or accessed across different geographical locations, depending on the platform's hosting configuration and technical requirements.

Where personal data is transferred outside Nigeria, Malaria Consortium will ensure that such transfers comply with the international data transfer requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Appropriate safeguards will be implemented to protect personal data during international transfers, including applicable contractual and security arrangements.

11. How We Protect Your Personal Data

Malaria Consortium implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction or misuse.

These measures include, as applicable:
• Restricted access to personal data based on authorised roles and responsibilities.
• User authentication and account access controls.
• Appropriate information security and confidentiality requirements.
• Secure storage and transmission arrangements.
• Monitoring of system access and relevant security events.
• Periodic review of access privileges and platform security arrangements.
• Appropriate safeguards for information stored through offline functionality.
• Staff and authorised users' awareness of applicable data protection and information security requirements.

Users are expected to protect their login credentials and devices and must not share passwords or allow unauthorised individuals to access their LMS accounts.

12. Data Retention and Disposal

Malaria Consortium retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, taking into consideration applicable legal, contractual, regulatory, project and organisational requirements.

LMS registration details, learning records, assessment results, platform interactions and related information will be retained only for as long as necessary for their approved purposes.

Financial and other records required for financial accountability will be retained for seven (7) years after the conclusion of the project, where required under applicable financial and organisational retention requirements.

Where personal data is no longer required for its original purpose, it will be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable retention and disposal requirements.

Where information is retained for historical, research, statistical or reporting purposes beyond its original retention period, it will be anonymised so that individuals are no longer identifiable.

13. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have rights concerning your personal data, subject to applicable legal conditions and exemptions.

These include:
• Right to be informed: To receive clear information about how and why your personal data is processed.
• Right of access: To request access to personal data held about you.
• Right to rectification: To request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
• Right to erasure: To request deletion of your personal data where applicable.
• Right to restriction: To request restriction of processing in circumstances provided by law.
• Right to data portability: To request a copy of your personal data in a commonly used and machine-readable format, where applicable.
• Right to object: To object to certain processing activities, including processing based on legitimate interests, where applicable.
• Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time.
• Right to lodge a complaint: To lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your personal data has been processed in violation of applicable data protection requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact the Malaria Consortium Data Protection Officer using the contact details provided in this notice.
